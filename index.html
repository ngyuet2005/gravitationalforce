01

為何衛星不會直線飛走？Why does a satellite not fly off in a straight line?

沒有外力時，物體會保持等速直線運動。衛星卻不斷轉彎，代表它必須有一個持續指向圓心的合力（向心力）。對理想圓形軌道而言，地球對衛星的萬有引力正正提供這個向心力。
Without a net force, an object travels in a straight line at constant velocity. A satellite keeps turning, so it needs a resultant force towards the centre (centripetal force). For an ideal circular orbit, gravity supplies this centripetal force.
Law of gravitation / 萬有引力定律
Fg = GMmr²
質量分別為 M 和 m，兩者質心距離為 r。
Masses M and m, separated by a centre-to-centre distance r.
Circular motion / 圓周運動
Fc = mv²r
衛星質量為 m，速率為 v，圓形軌道半徑為 r。
The satellite has mass m, speed v and orbital radius r.
最重要一句：「向心力」不是另一種額外的力，而是描述指向圓心的合力所扮演的角色。因此不能在受力圖中同時畫「萬有引力」及一個額外的「向心力」。
Key idea: Centripetal force is not an additional force. It describes the inward resultant force. Do not draw gravity and a separate “centripetal force” as two forces on the same free-body diagram.

03

由兩條公式推導三個 DSE 結果Derive three key HKDSE results

假設只有萬有引力提供向心力，將兩條公式相等：
Assume gravity is the only force providing the centripetal force. Equate the two expressions:
GMmr² = mv²r
兩邊約去衛星質量 m，再乘以 r：
Cancel satellite mass m, then multiply both sides by r:
v² = GMr   ⇒   v = √(GM/r)
圓周運動一圈的路程是 2πr，所以週期 T = 2πr/v。把上式代入：
One complete revolution covers 2πr, so T = 2πr/v. Substitute the speed expression:
T = 2π√(r³/GM)
T² = 4π²GMr³   ⇒   T² ∝ r³
容易失分：這裡的 r 是地心至衛星的距離，而不是離地高度。r = R + h。另外，上述結果假設理想圓形軌道。
Common mistake: r is measured from Earth's centre, not from its surface. r = R + h. These results assume an ideal circular orbit.
Circular orbital speed / 圓軌道速率
v = √(GM/r)
Orbital period / 公轉週期
T = 2π√(r³/GM)
Kepler's third law / 開普勒第三定律
T² ∝ r³
Orbital radius / 軌道半徑
r = R + h
符號與常數說明Symbols and constants

G = 6.67 × 10−11 N m² kg−2; M = 5.97 × 1024 kg (Earth's mass); R = 6.37 × 106 m (Earth's radius); m satellite mass (kg); r orbital radius (m); h altitude (m); v speed (m s−1); T period (s).

DSE 式分步例題Step-by-step exam-style examples

Example 1 · 基礎計算 / Basic calculation
一枚質量為 800 kg 的衛星在距地面 630 km 的圓形軌道運行。計算其軌道速率及週期。
A satellite of mass 800 kg moves in a circular orbit 630 km above Earth's surface. Calculate its orbital speed and period.
1
先找地心距離（換算 km → m）
Find the centre-to-centre radius (convert km → m)
r = 6.37 × 10⁶ + 6.30 × 10⁵ = 7.00 × 10⁶ m
2
用圓軌道速率公式，衛星質量不需要代入
Use the circular-orbit speed formula; satellite mass is not needed
v = √(GM/r) = 7.54 × 10³ m s−1
3
由一圈路程除以速率求週期
Divide the circumference by the speed to obtain the period
T = 2πrv = 5.83 × 10³ s ≈ 97.2 min
Example 2 · 比例推理 / Ratio question
衛星 B 的圓形軌道半徑是衛星 A 的 4 倍，兩者均繞同一行星運行。求 vB/vA 及 TB/TA。
Satellite B orbits the same planet at 4 times the orbital radius of satellite A. Find vB/vA and TB/TA.
v ∝ r−1/2   ⇒   vBvA = 4−1/2 = 1/2
T ∝ r3/2   ⇒   TBTA = 43/2 = 8
結論：軌道半徑較大 → 速率較小，但週期更長。
Conclusion: A larger orbital radius gives a lower orbital speed but a longer orbital period.

即時練習：選擇 + 結構題Instant practice: MC + structured questions

Structured question · 結構題
兩枚衛星 X、Y 繞同一行星作圓周運動，兩者的質量比 mX : mY = 2 : 1，軌道半徑相同。求 (a) 速率比 vX : vY；(b) 所受萬有引力比 FX : FY。請解釋原因。（4 分）
Satellites X and Y orbit the same planet in circles of equal radius. Their masses are in the ratio mX : mY = 2 : 1. Find (a) their speed ratio vX : vY; (b) their gravitational-force ratio FX : FY. Explain your answers. (4 marks)
(a) 1 : 1。由 v = √(GM/r)，速率與衛星質量無關。（2 分）
(b) 2 : 1。由 F = GMm/r²，在相同 M、r 下，F ∝ m。（2 分）
(a) 1 : 1. From v = √(GM/r), orbital speed is independent of satellite mass. (2 marks)
(b) 2 : 1. From F = GMm/r², at the same M and r, F ∝ m. (2 marks)

考試常見陷阱Common exam pitfalls

① r ≠ h
公式用地心距離 r = R + h，不是高度 h。
Use orbital radius r = R + h, not altitude h.
② Fc is not an extra force
不要把重力與「向心力」當兩個獨立的力相加。
Do not add gravity to a fictitious separate centripetal force.
③ v ≠ constant vector
等速圓周運動的速率不變，但速度方向持續改變，所以有加速度。
In uniform circular motion, speed is constant but velocity changes direction; acceleration is non-zero.
④ m cancels only for orbital v and T
衛星質量不影響理想圓軌道的 v、T，但影響它所受的 Fg。
Satellite mass does not affect ideal circular-orbit v or T, but does affect Fg.
DSE 答題句式：「由於萬有引力提供衛星作圓周運動所需的向心力，因此 GMm/r² = mv²/r。」先寫出物理關係，再代數運算。
Exam answer wording: “Since the gravitational force provides the centripetal force required for the satellite's circular motion, GMm/r² = mv²/r.” State the physical principle before doing the algebra.
